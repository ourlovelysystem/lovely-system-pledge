# History 011 — Proof as a market signal RFC

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Status:** Proposed
**Specification:** N/A
**Supersedes:** None
**Related:** [RFC 0004 — Proof as a Market Signal](../rfcs/0004-proof-as-a-market-signal.md), [RFC 0001 — Capital Disposition Voting Test](../rfcs/0001-capital-disposition-voting-test.md), [RFC 0003 — Ours to Give](../rfcs/0003-ours-to-give.md)

## Context

The operator proposed:

> By proving Our Lovely System we add value to Our Lovely System.

The operator then corrected a possible self-certifying interpretation:

> The outcome of proving Our Lovely System is not the statement "We have proved Our Lovely System".

The operator supplied the market relationship:

> Proof is a market signal.

The operator directed:

> Write it up. Include in character hostile review. Log it.

## Direction

Pledge should treat proof as a bounded test that produces evidence and consequence. Evidence enters a human market of judgment and changes what people are willing to risk, lend, contribute, accept, honor, challenge, or carry.

The system must not convert bounded evidence into a global declaration that Our Lovely System has been proved.

## Decisions

- The proposal is recorded as RFC 0004.
- Proof is bounded to a claim, conditions, consequence, and surviving evidence.
- Proof is a market signal rather than a self-awarded certificate.
- Success, failure, correction, and concealment have different evidentiary and value consequences.
- Our Lovely System may not serve as the sole judge that Our Lovely System has been proved.
- Market response is not automatically truth, trust, consent, virtue, or moral authority.
- Voting and Ours to Give are identified as candidate proof features.
- The requested in-character hostile review is included and attributed to Codex performing the Oracle of Snake Mountain character.

## Corrections

The phrase “proving Our Lovely System” does not mean that the system becomes globally or permanently proved. A proof record should describe the bounded claim, conditions, result, evidence, and limitations.

## Open questions

RFC 0004 preserves open questions concerning verification constituencies, conflicting signals, proof cost, refusal and exit, independence, proof retirement, and additional proof features.

## Result

RFC 0004 is prepared as a public proposal with an in-character hostile review and response.

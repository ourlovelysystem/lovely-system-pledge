# History 010 — Ours to Give supersedes IOU Bank

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Status:** Accepted direction
**Specification:** N/A
**Supersedes:** [History 009 — IOU Bank RFC](./2026-08-29-009-iou-bank-rfc.md)
**Related:** [RFC 0003 — Ours to Give](../rfcs/0003-ours-to-give.md), [RFC 0002 — IOU Bank](../rfcs/0002-iou-bank.md), [RFC 0001 — Capital Disposition Voting Test](../rfcs/0001-capital-disposition-voting-test.md)

## Context

After publication of RFC 0002, the operator explored names based on IOU language and identified that both **I Owe Our Lovely System** and **I Owe Us** reinforced an unwanted debt relationship or separateness.

The operator supplied the intended expression:

> This is my body which is given to you.

The operator then proposed:

> This was mine to give. Now it's ours to give. How about an Ours to Give pledge. OTG?

After evaluation, the operator declared:

> Agreed. Write it up. Post it. Pledge and Computahhh.

## Direction

**Ours to Give**, abbreviated **OTG**, replaces the IOU Bank framing.

An OTG is an offering rather than a debt instrument. The writer gives the artifact and proposed opportunity into the collective. An accepter may carry it forward. When honored, its stated capital becomes ours to give.

## Decisions

- The name is **Ours to Give**.
- The abbreviation is **OTG**.
- The governing expression is: **“This was mine to give. Now it's ours to give.”**
- The artifact is an **Ours to Give pledge** or **OTG pledge**.
- The writer is not framed as debtor to an external Our Lovely System.
- The accepter does not collect a debt from the writer.
- Submission gives the written artifact; honoring supplies the stated capital.
- Proposed value remains distinct from capital received.
- The market actions and collective-human-judgment model from RFC 0002 are preserved under the OTG framing.
- RFC 0003 supersedes RFC 0002.
- Honored OTG capital may later become governed by the Capital Disposition Voting Test under separately declared terms.

## Corrections

RFC 0002's IOU framing is superseded. It remains public as evidence of the prior proposal and the correction that followed.

## Open questions

RFC 0003 preserves open questions concerning amounts, acknowledgment behavior, eligibility, acceptance release, completion evidence, credit, inspection, voting standing, and the handling of actual capital.

## Result

RFC 0003 records Ours to Give as accepted naming and governing direction while leaving implementation proposed.

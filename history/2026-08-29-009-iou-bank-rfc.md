# History 009 — IOU Bank RFC

**Date:** 2026-08-29
**Status:** Proposed
**Specification:** N/A
**Supersedes:** None
**Related:** [RFC 0002 — IOU Bank](../rfcs/0002-iou-bank.md), [RFC 0001 — Capital Disposition Voting Test](../rfcs/0001-capital-disposition-voting-test.md)

## Context

The operator proposed a test capital generator in which a user submits a written artifact such as:

> **IOU 5**

The template contains a name field explicitly marked optional. The writer chooses the amount and manner of self-attribution. Other users may shop the notes, accept one, supply its stated capital, and receive credit for honoring it. An accepter may act anonymously, with self-identification, or as a system-knowable user.

The operator corrected an initial interpretation that treated the note primarily as an assignable obligation:

> The writer creates a marketable item. The writer chooses the amount and chooses their own manner of self attribution. Collective human judgement determines which notes get accepted and which notes get a Yeah, okay. 1,000,000 contributed by the Master of Disaster? Let's not honor that one.

The operator then directed:

> Write it up and post it.

## Direction

Pledge should explore an IOU Bank in which a writer proposes value through a written marketable item and collective human judgment determines which notes receive acknowledgment, acceptance, and actual honoring.

## Decisions

- The proposal is recorded as RFC 0002.
- Writer-selected value is proposed value, not capital received.
- The written IOU artifact remains part of the contribution record.
- The name field is visibly optional.
- **Yeah, okay** is an acknowledgment signal distinct from acceptance.
- Users shop notes before voluntarily accepting responsibility for one.
- Writer attribution and accepter attribution are independently chosen.
- Offered, acknowledged, accepted, honored, and credited evidence remain distinguishable.
- A large implausible note may increase the offered balance without increasing accepted or honored capital.
- RFC 0002 may generate capital for RFC 0001 only under separately declared terms.

## Corrections

The IOU note is not characterized as an obligation indiscriminately assigned by the bank. The writer creates a marketable item. Human judgment determines which items are acknowledged, accepted, and honored.

## Open questions

RFC 0002 preserves open questions concerning units, acknowledgment rules, eligibility, acceptance duration and release, payment failure, completion evidence, contribution credit, receipts, suspicious signals, metadata inspection, and legal or payment classification.

## Result

RFC 0002 is prepared as a public proposal for the IOU Bank.
